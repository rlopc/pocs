# Sentence Transformation – *A Day I Will Never Forget*

Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence. Use the word given. **Do not change the word given.** Use between **two and five words**, including the word given.

1. I wanted to escape the noise of the city.

   **ORDER**

   I went to the village _____ the noise of the city.

2. I had always enjoyed visiting quiet places.

   **LIKED**

   I had always _____ quiet places.

3. The owner allowed me to leave my bags in my room.

   **LET**

   The owner _____ my bags in my room.

4. She said, "You should walk to the lake."

   **SUGGESTED**

   She _____ to the lake.

5. I promised that I would be careful.

   **PROMISED**

   I _____ careful.

6. I loved hearing the birds.

   **ENJOYED**

   I _____ the birds.

7. I forgot to bring my phone.

   **REMEMBER**

   I didn't _____ my phone.

8. I decided not to turn back.

   **KEPT**

   I _____ instead of turning back.

9. I wanted to reach the lake before sunset.

   **HOPED**

   I _____ the lake before sunset.

10. I could not find the path.

    **ABLE**

    I _____ the path.

11. I offered to help the young man.

    **OFFERED**

    I _____ the young man.

12. He said, "Let's climb the hill."

    **SUGGESTED**

    He _____ the hill.

13. I told him to stay where he was.

    **TOLD**

    I _____ where he was.

14. The elderly woman allowed me to use her phone.

    **LET**

    The elderly woman _____ her phone.

15. The rescue team made me show them the way.

    **MADE**

    The rescue team _____ them the way.

16. I have stopped going into unfamiliar places without a phone.

    **GIVEN**

    I _____ into unfamiliar places without a phone.

17. I have learned to follow advice.

    **LEARNED**

    I _____ advice.

18. I will never forget helping someone that day.

    **ALWAYS**

    I _____ helping someone that day.

19. That experience taught me to be more responsible.

    **LEARNED**

    I _____ more responsible because of that experience.

20. The experience completely changed my way of thinking.

    **MADE**

    The experience _____ differently.
