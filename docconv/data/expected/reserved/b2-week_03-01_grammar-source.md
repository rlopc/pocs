# Erasmus stories in nine grammar forms (B2+)

**★ In the present continuous, always say what they are wearing.**

|  | PRESENT SIMPLE = Habit | PRESENT CONTINUOUS = Right now | PAST SIMPLE = Finished | PRESENT PERFECT = Indeterminate | PRESENT PERFECT = Starts in past and continues | PRESENT CONTINUOUS FUTURE = Programmed future | BE GOING TO = Open future | WILL = Promises and instant decisions | MAY / MIGHT = Future possibility |
