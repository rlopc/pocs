# B2 Sentence transformation

Complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence. Use **2–5 words**, including the word given. **Do not change the word given.**

1. He normally checks his passport before every trip.

   **USUALLY**

   He _____ his passport before every trip.

2. She is having dinner with her host family at 8 tonight.

   **MEETING**

   She _____ her host family for dinner at 8 tonight.

3. He has flown alone before.

   **FIRST**

   This is not _____ flown alone.

4. This is the first time she has tried local food.

   **NEVER**

   She _____ local food before.

5. He started using this metro route two weeks ago.

   **FOR**

   He _____ this metro route for two weeks.

6. They arranged to start science class at 11 o’clock.

   **STARTING**

   They _____ science class at 11 o’clock.

7. She intends to buy a small thank-you gift.

   **GOING**

   She _____ a small thank-you gift.

8. He promises to wash the dishes.

   **WILL**

   He says, “I _____ the dishes.”

9. Perhaps they will watch a film together tonight.

   **MIGHT**

   They _____ a film together tonight.

10. They started living in the residence a month ago.

    **BEEN**

    They _____ in the residence for a month.

11. She goes shopping for school supplies every Saturday.

    **USUALLY**

    She _____ school supplies on Saturdays.

12. At the moment, she is looking for a notebook.

    **NOW**

    She _____ a notebook right now.

13. They found their classrooms after they got their schedules.

    **BEFORE**

    They got their schedules _____ their classrooms.

14. He has never travelled alone before.

    **FIRST**

    This is _____ travelled alone.

15. They arranged to give the presentation tomorrow morning.

    **GIVING**

    They _____ the presentation tomorrow morning.

16. He intends to visit the Alhambra tomorrow.

    **GOING**

    He _____ the Alhambra tomorrow.

17. “I’ll send you the photos,” he said.

    **PROMISED**

    He _____ me the photos.

18. Perhaps the bus will be late because of the rain.

    **MIGHT**

    The bus _____ because of the rain.

19. She started having lunch in this canteen in September and still does.

    **SINCE**

    She _____ in this canteen since September.

20. They often revise together after school.

    **TEND**

    They _____ together after school.

4. **has never tried**

6. **are starting**
7. **is going to buy**
8. **will wash**
9. **might watch**
10. **have lived**
11. **usually shops for**
12. **is looking for**
13. **before finding**
14. **the first time he has**
15. **are giving**
16. **is going to visit**
17. **promised to send**
18. **might be late**
19. **has had lunch / has been having (because of repetition)**
