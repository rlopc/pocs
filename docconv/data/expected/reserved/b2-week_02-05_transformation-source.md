# Sentence Transformation

**Little Red Riding Hood: Grandma’s Secret | B2**

   Mum _____ Grandma garlic bread.

2. Grandma asks Red, “Are you on your way?”

3. Grandma became a vampire in 1823 and is still one.

   SINCE

   Grandma _____ 1823.

4. Grandma no longer bites people.

   GIVEN

   Grandma _____ people.

5. Grandma has always taken good care of her skin.

   Grandma has always _____ her skin.

   Mum _____ some groceries to Grandma.
