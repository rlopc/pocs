# A Day I Will Never Forget

Last summer, I decided **to spend** a weekend in a small mountain village because I wanted **to escape** the noise of the city. I had always enjoyed **visiting** quiet places, and I hoped **to relax**, **to explore** the area, and **to forget** about work for a few days.

When I arrived, the owner of the guest house let me **leave** my bags in my room before checking in. She suggested **walking** to a nearby lake and warned me not **to go** too far into the forest. I promised **to be** careful, but I was too excited **to listen** properly.

I started **following** a narrow path through the trees. I loved **hearing** the birds, **watching** the sunlight move through the leaves, and **breathing** the fresh mountain air. After **walking** for nearly an hour, I stopped **to drink** some water. That was when I realised that I had forgotten **to bring** my phone.

I considered **turning** back, but I decided **to continue**. I wanted **to reach** the lake before sunset, and I thought I could **find** the way without using a map. However, the path suddenly disappeared, and I became completely lost.

A few minutes later, I heard someone **calling** for help. I followed the voice and found a young man sitting beside a fallen tree. He had injured his ankle and could not **stand** up. I offered **to help** him, and together we tried **to find** the main path. He suggested **climbing** a small hill so that we could **see** the village, but his ankle was too painful **to move**.

I told him **to stay** where he was while I went **to look** for help. I had never felt so frightened, but I forced myself **to remain** calm. After **walking** for another twenty minutes, I saw a small cabin. An elderly woman opened the door and let me **use** her phone. She called the emergency services and asked me **to explain** exactly where the injured man was.

The rescue team arrived quickly. They made me **show** them the way, and soon the young man was found and taken to hospital. I was exhausted, relieved, and amazed that everything had ended safely.

Since that day, I have stopped **going** into unfamiliar places without **taking** a phone or a map. I have also learned **to follow** advice instead of **assuming** that nothing will go wrong. That experience taught me **to be** more responsible, and I will never forget **helping** someone when they truly needed me.
